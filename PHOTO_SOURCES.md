## Logo font

Allura by Robert E. Leuschke, downloaded from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Allura). Font file and SIL Open Font License are in `client/public/fonts`. The font contains the accented é used in BonAppé Find.

## Reading fonts

Lato and Cormorant Garamond are also bundled locally from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included in `client/public/fonts`. Font definitions are in `client/src/assets/css/fonts.css`. The application does not need Google Fonts requests for its typography.
